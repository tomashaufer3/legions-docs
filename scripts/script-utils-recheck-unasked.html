How it works

The route asked Opus about a claim — this part has a fire brigade — when the readers disagreed on it, and on German pages only about the whole entry (D-118: the box locator failed its gate). A mark where the readers read different things, or where one reader read nothing, was never asked when the claims came out even; the ledger gives it the reader of record's reading and the rule not asked: reader of record. The 15b validation sample found about half of those wrong in each German volume, and the Böhmen gold 92 of 255. This asks Opus about each of them, with §6's call (opus_pass.build_call: the whole glyph outlined, no reading shown), with RULES and how the offered marks built on another glyph are printed (rules_text) after the question:

  • an outlined mark some reader named a symbol at — which is printed there?, offered the readers' symbols and their look-alikes (run_verify_test_b.options_for), none of these and cannot tell;
  • a claim on the whole entry (no box) — is it printed for this part?, shown the part's sections (the list of parts and the offices, as the route shows a part, D-130).

A mark where no reader named a symbol is not asked: the route already says nothing is printed.

The follow-up (D-195): a box can sit off the glyph (on the German pages the locator puts boxes on entry numbers, Roman numerals and letters), so none of these or cannot tell at a box says nothing about the claim. Every symbol a reader named at such a box is then asked of the whole entry (followup_items, stages STAGE_FOLLOW_GOLD and STAGE_FOLLOW), with its own gold test. The gold is transcribed by entry, not by box, so the gold test judges a box answer on its part (gold_rows). The Czech volume adds its opus settled nothing; readers agree marks (4 of 20 right in 15b).

German pages are asked of the whole entry (D-196). On the German volumes a box answer is right about the box and wrong about the claim far too often: judged against the Czech volume, §6's box answers changed 223 German Böhmen claims and 211 of the changes were wrong (the box sat on the neighbouring glyph or the line above, D-118). So on a German page every outlined mark of the group becomes claims on its part — every symbol a reader named there, the route's value, and their look-alikes (the same glyph in brackets or with a letter, composites; the hand-check pairs) — each asked is it printed for this part? with the part's sections in view (D-118c). The Czech volume, whose boxes passed the locator's gate, keeps the box question. reask_items asks the same of every German mark §6 put to Opus where Opus named something other than the route.

The tests, before any pass (D-190's bars, opus_pass.BAR; passed):

  • the gold (STAGE_GOLD): every such mark on the Böhmen and Czech gold pages, scored against the author's transcription (the gold run's gold column). Reference cuts never come from the asked mark's own page (reference_card), so no mark is shown its own glyph.
  • the Czech volume (STAGE_EDITIONS, German pages, D-196): the entry questions on EDITIONS_PARTS German Böhmen parts drawn from the pass and the re-ask (paired one to one with a Czech municipality, never a sealed, reserve or gold page), each answer judged by what the Czech volume prints (editions_check), institutions and place types as two groups (the route agrees with the Czech volume on 0.926 of the first and 0.81 of the second, where the editions may also list a municipality's parts differently). Out of sample, and at the level the tables are kept.
  • your rulings (STAGE_HAND, a diagnostic since D-196: it tests the box, not the claim): 60 Mähren and 60 Schlesien marks drawn from this group on the full run — never on sealed, reserve or calibration pages, never in the 15b validation sample (kept as the check after the run) — ruled blind on the Unasked Marks page, offered what Opus is offered. The key is reference/places/unasked_checks/hand_key.csv.

The pass (STAGE_PASS): one call per part over the full runs, leaving out the marks §6 already asked. merge applies it as step §6d, only where both tests passed (passed).

Everything goes under output/recheck/&lt;edition&gt;/&lt;stage&gt;/; nothing is sent without spend, and only on the machine of record.

def rules_text(questions: list[dict]) -> str:
    """:data:`RULES` (the outline rule only where a mark is outlined), then how each offered or asked
    mark built on another glyph is printed."""
    built = composites()
    offered = {t for q in questions for t in q.get("options", [q.get("token")]) if t}
    bases = {built[t][0] for t in offered if t in built} | (offered & {b for b, _h in built.values()})
    lines = [f"- {t} is {how}." for t, (b, how) in sorted(built.items()) if b in bases]
    head = RULES % (BOX_RULE if any(q.get("kind") == "mark" for q in questions) else "")

def group(edition: str, pages: str, skip: set = frozenset()) -> tuple[list[dict], list[dict]]:
    """``(marks, claims)`` of one run in the group: outlined marks some reader named a symbol at
    (``{mark, part, image, box, placed, dino, route, gold}``) and claims on the whole entry
    (``{mark, part, token, route, gold}``). ``skip`` — marks asked elsewhere."""
    run = rf.out_dir(edition, pages)
    rows = _read(run / "ledger" / f"marks_{edition}_{pages}.csv")
    named = defaultdict(list)
    for e in _read(run / "ledger" / f"ledger_{edition}_{pages}.csv"):
        if e["step"] == "read" and e["read"].startswith("[") and e["read"] not in named[e["mark"]]:
            named[e["mark"]].append(e["read"])
    record = rf.labels(pages, edition)["gemini"]
    verdicts = rf.verdicts_path(edition, pages)
    dino = ({led.box_mark(r["image"], r["box"]): r["dino"] for r in _read(verdicts)
             if r["pass"] == record and r["box"]} if Path(verdicts).exists() else {})
    marks, claims = [], []
    for m in rows:
        if not in_group(m["rule"], edition) or m["mark"] in skip:
            continue
        part = (m["page"], m["district"], str(m["muni"]), str(m["seq"]))
        if _boxed(m["mark"]):
            if not named.get(m["mark"]):
                continue
            image, box = op._mark_box(m["mark"])
            seen = dino.get(m["mark"], "")
            marks.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": part, "image": image, "box": box,
                          "placed": named[m["mark"]], "dino": [seen] if seen.startswith("[") else [],
                          "route": m["final"], "gold": m.get("gold", "")})
        else:
            claims.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": part, "token": m["mark"].split("#")[1],
                           "route": m["final"], "gold": m.get("gold", "")})
    return marks, claims

def look_alikes(edition: str) -> dict:
    """``{token: partners}``: the same glyph in brackets or with a letter (:func:`composites`) and the
    hand-check pairs (castle and poorhouse, gamekeeper and huntsman)."""
    by_base = defaultdict(set)
    for token, (base, _how) in composites().items():

        for token in family:
            partners[token] |= family - {token}
    if PAIRS.exists():

            if g["edition"] == edition:
                partners[g["a"]].add(g["b"])
                partners[g["b"]].add(g["a"])
    return partners

def as_entry_claims(marks: list[dict], claims: list[dict], edition: str, pages: str,
                    vocabulary: set | None = None) -> list[dict]:
    """A German run's outlined marks as claims on their parts (D-196): every symbol a reader named at
    the mark, the route's value there and their look-alikes, each ``{mark, part, token, route, gold}``
    with ``route`` the token where the run's tables hold it on the part. The run's own entry claims
    come first; no (part, token) twice."""
    held, partners = held_sets(edition, pages), look_alikes(edition)
    vocabulary = vocabulary if vocabulary is not None else set(rf._english(edition))
    out, seen = [], set()
    for c in claims:
        if (c["part"], c["token"]) not in seen:
            seen.add((c["part"], c["token"]))
            out.append(c)
    for m in marks:
        named = set(m["placed"]) | ({m["route"]} if m["route"].startswith("[") else set())
        for token in sorted(named | {p for t in named for p in partners.get(t, ())}):
            key = (m["part"], token)
            if token not in vocabulary or key in seen:
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            out.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": m["part"], "token": token,
                        "route": token if token in held.get(m["part"], set()) else led.ABSENT, "gold": ""})
    return out

def calls(marks: list[dict], claims: list[dict], ctx: dict, prefix: str, one_per_mark: bool = False) -> list[dict]:
    """One call per part (or per mark): its outlined marks asked *which is printed there?*, its
    entry claims *is it printed for this part?* with the part's sections in view."""
    by_part = {}
    for m in marks:
        key = m["mark"] if one_per_mark else m["part"]
        by_part.setdefault(key, {"part": m["part"], "marks": [], "tokens": set()})["marks"].append(m)
    for c in claims:
        key = c["mark"] if one_per_mark else c["part"]
        by_part.setdefault(key, {"part": c["part"], "marks": [], "tokens": set()})["tokens"].add(c["token"])
    items = []
    for key in sorted(by_part):
        p = by_part[key]
        part = p["part"]
        regions = ([(name, "", "") for name in tb._section_list(part[0], part[1], part[3])] if p["tokens"] else [])
        uid = tb.unit_id(part) if not one_per_mark else f"{tb.unit_id(part)}-{len(items) + 1:04d}"
        items.append(with_rules(op.build_call(f"{prefix}-{uid}", part, p["marks"], sorted(p["tokens"]), ctx, regions)))
    return items

def score(rows: list[dict]) -> list[dict]:
    """Per group, D-190's bars: ``rows`` — ``{group, truth, route, answer}`` with answer on the
    ledger's scale (*cannot tell* is a disagreement and changes nothing), or already judged
    ``{group, right, route_right, changed, empty}``. An *empty* row (a gold box Opus found empty,
    left to the follow-up) is counted apart and not in the share."""
    out = []
    groups = sorted({r["group"] for r in rows})
    rows = [_judged(r) for r in rows if r.get("answer", "") is not None]   # not answered: not asked
    for name in groups:
        mine = [r for r in rows if r["group"] == name]
        asked = [r for r in mine if not r.get("empty")]
        agree = sum(r["right"] for r in asked)
        confirmed = [r for r in asked if r["route_right"]]
        changed = sum(r["changed"] for r in confirmed)
        n = len(asked)
        low = op.wilson_low(agree, n)
        judged = n >= op.BAR["min_group"]
        out.append({"group": name, "n": n, "empty": len(mine) - n, "agree": agree, "share": round(agree / n, 3) if n else None,
                    "wilson_low": round(low, 3), "confirmed": len(confirmed), "changed": changed,
                    "change_share": round(changed / len(confirmed), 3) if confirmed else None,
                    "passes": (not judged) or (agree / n >= op.BAR["agree"] and low >= op.BAR["wilson_low"]
                                               and (not confirmed or changed / len(confirmed) <= op.BAR["change_max"])),
                    "judged": judged})
    return out

def composites() -> dict[str, tuple[str, str]]:
    """``{token: (base, how it is built)}`` for the plate's marks built on another glyph."""
    rows = {r["token"]: (r["base"], r["brackets"], r["letter"], "") for r in _read(COMPOSITES)}
    rows.update(EXTRA_COMPOSITES)
    out = {}
    for token, (base, brackets, letter, extra) in rows.items():
        how = [BRACKETS[brackets]] if brackets else []
        how += [f"with the letter {letter} beside it"] if letter else []
        how += [f"with {extra} beside it"] if extra else []
        out[token] = (base, f"{base} " + ", ".join(how))
    return out


def rules_text(questions: list[dict]) -> str:
    """:data:`RULES` (the outline rule only where a mark is outlined), then how each offered or asked
    mark built on another glyph is printed."""
    built = composites()
    offered = {t for q in questions for t in q.get("options", [q.get("token")]) if t}
    bases = {built[t][0] for t in offered if t in built} | (offered & {b for b, _h in built.values()})
    lines = [f"- {t} is {how}." for t, (b, how) in sorted(built.items()) if b in bases]
    head = RULES % (BOX_RULE if any(q.get("kind") == "mark" for q in questions) else "")
    return head + ("\nMarks built on another glyph, among this question's options:\n" + "\n".join(lines)
                   if lines else "")


def with_rules(item: dict) -> dict:
    item["question"] += rules_text(item["questions"])
    return item

    tb.BATCHES, tb.REPORT = folder / "batches.json", folder / "report.txt"
    tb.BOXES = True
    return folder

def in_group(rule: str, edition: str) -> bool:
    """The ledger rules this run asks about: the reader of record's, and in the Czech volume the
    marks Opus was asked about and settled nothing."""
    return rule == NOT_ASKED or (edition == "cz" and rule.startswith(SETTLED_NOTHING))


def group(edition: str, pages: str, skip: set = frozenset()) -> tuple[list[dict], list[dict]]:
    """``(marks, claims)`` of one run in the group: outlined marks some reader named a symbol at
    (``{mark, part, image, box, placed, dino, route, gold}``) and claims on the whole entry
    (``{mark, part, token, route, gold}``). ``skip`` — marks asked elsewhere."""
    run = rf.out_dir(edition, pages)
    rows = _read(run / "ledger" / f"marks_{edition}_{pages}.csv")
    named = defaultdict(list)
    for e in _read(run / "ledger" / f"ledger_{edition}_{pages}.csv"):
        if e["step"] == "read" and e["read"].startswith("[") and e["read"] not in named[e["mark"]]:
            named[e["mark"]].append(e["read"])
    record = rf.labels(pages, edition)["gemini"]
    verdicts = rf.verdicts_path(edition, pages)
    dino = ({led.box_mark(r["image"], r["box"]): r["dino"] for r in _read(verdicts)
             if r["pass"] == record and r["box"]} if Path(verdicts).exists() else {})
    marks, claims = [], []
    for m in rows:
        if not in_group(m["rule"], edition) or m["mark"] in skip:
            continue
        part = (m["page"], m["district"], str(m["muni"]), str(m["seq"]))
        if _boxed(m["mark"]):
            if not named.get(m["mark"]):
                continue
            image, box = op._mark_box(m["mark"])
            seen = dino.get(m["mark"], "")
            marks.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": part, "image": image, "box": box,
                          "placed": named[m["mark"]], "dino": [seen] if seen.startswith("[") else [],
                          "route": m["final"], "gold": m.get("gold", "")})
        else:
            claims.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": part, "token": m["mark"].split("#")[1],
                           "route": m["final"], "gold": m.get("gold", "")})
    return marks, claims


def asked_by_pass(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> set:
    """The outlined marks §6's pass already put to Opus."""
    path = Path(out) / edition / "opus_pass" / "items.json"
    if not path.exists():
        return set()
    return {led.box_mark(q["image"], ",".join(map(str, q["box"])))
            for it in json.loads(path.read_text("utf-8")) for q in it["questions"] if q["kind"] == "mark"}


def look_alikes(edition: str) -> dict:
    """``{token: partners}``: the same glyph in brackets or with a letter (:func:`composites`) and the
    hand-check pairs (castle and poorhouse, gamekeeper and huntsman)."""
    by_base = defaultdict(set)
    for token, (base, _how) in composites().items():
        by_base[base] |= {base, token}
    partners = defaultdict(set)
    for family in by_base.values():
        for token in family:
            partners[token] |= family - {token}
    if PAIRS.exists():
        for g in json.loads(PAIRS.read_text("utf-8")):
            if g["edition"] == edition:
                partners[g["a"]].add(g["b"])
                partners[g["b"]].add(g["a"])
    return partners


def held_sets(edition: str, pages: str) -> dict:
    """``{part: codes}`` of the run's own tables (the inventory and the types)."""
    run = rf.out_dir(edition, pages)
    held = defaultdict(set)
    for name in ("final_inventory.csv", "final_types.csv"):
        if (run / name).exists():
            for r in _read(run / name):
                held[(r["page"], r["district"], str(r["muni_seq"]), str(r["settlement_seq"]))].add(r["code"])
    return held


def as_entry_claims(marks: list[dict], claims: list[dict], edition: str, pages: str,
                    vocabulary: set | None = None) -> list[dict]:
    """A German run's outlined marks as claims on their parts (D-196): every symbol a reader named at
    the mark, the route's value there and their look-alikes, each ``{mark, part, token, route, gold}``
    with ``route`` the token where the run's tables hold it on the part. The run's own entry claims
    come first; no (part, token) twice."""
    held, partners = held_sets(edition, pages), look_alikes(edition)
    vocabulary = vocabulary if vocabulary is not None else set(rf._english(edition))
    out, seen = [], set()
    for c in claims:
        if (c["part"], c["token"]) not in seen:
            seen.add((c["part"], c["token"]))
            out.append(c)
    for m in marks:
        named = set(m["placed"]) | ({m["route"]} if m["route"].startswith("[") else set())
        for token in sorted(named | {p for t in named for p in partners.get(t, ())}):
            key = (m["part"], token)
            if token not in vocabulary or key in seen:
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            out.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": m["part"], "token": token,
                        "route": token if token in held.get(m["part"], set()) else led.ABSENT, "gold": ""})
    return out


def stage_parts(edition: str, pages: str, skip: set = frozenset()) -> tuple[list[dict], list[dict]]:
    """``(marks, claims)`` to ask: a German run's marks as entry claims (D-196), the Czech one's as
    they are (its boxes passed the gate)."""
    marks, claims = group(edition, pages, skip)
    if edition in GERMAN:
        return [], as_entry_claims(marks, claims, edition, pages)
    return marks, claims

def calls(marks: list[dict], claims: list[dict], ctx: dict, prefix: str, one_per_mark: bool = False) -> list[dict]:
    """One call per part (or per mark): its outlined marks asked *which is printed there?*, its
    entry claims *is it printed for this part?* with the part's sections in view."""
    by_part = {}
    for m in marks:
        key = m["mark"] if one_per_mark else m["part"]
        by_part.setdefault(key, {"part": m["part"], "marks": [], "tokens": set()})["marks"].append(m)
    for c in claims:
        key = c["mark"] if one_per_mark else c["part"]
        by_part.setdefault(key, {"part": c["part"], "marks": [], "tokens": set()})["tokens"].add(c["token"])
    items = []
    for key in sorted(by_part):
        p = by_part[key]
        part = p["part"]
        regions = ([(name, "", "") for name in tb._section_list(part[0], part[1], part[3])] if p["tokens"] else [])
        uid = tb.unit_id(part) if not one_per_mark else f"{tb.unit_id(part)}-{len(items) + 1:04d}"
        items.append(with_rules(op.build_call(f"{prefix}-{uid}", part, p["marks"], sorted(p["tokens"]), ctx, regions)))
    return items


def gold_items(edition: str) -> list[dict]:
    marks, claims = stage_parts(edition, "gold")
    return calls(marks, claims, context(edition, "gold"), "g")


def hand_key() -> list[dict]:
    return _read(HAND_KEY) if HAND_KEY.exists() else []


def hand_items(edition: str) -> list[dict]:
    """One call per drawn mark, offered what the page offered you."""
    ctx = context(edition, "full")
    items = []
    for k in (k for k in hand_key() if k["edition"] == edition):
        image, box = op._mark_box(k["mark"])
        mark = {"mark": k["mark"], "part": (k["page"], k["district"], k["muni"], k["seq"]), "image": image,
                "box": box, "placed": k["readers"].split(" | "), "dino": [k["dino"]] if k["dino"].startswith("[") else []}
        items.append(with_rules(op.build_call(f"h-{k['id']}", mark["part"], [mark], [], ctx)))
    return items


def pass_claims(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> tuple[list[dict], list[dict]]:
    """``(marks, claims)`` of the pass: the group less the marks §6 asked; on a German run, less the
    claims the re-ask (:func:`reask_claims`) puts."""
    marks, claims = stage_parts(edition, "full", skip=asked_by_pass(edition, out))
    if edition in GERMAN:
        reasked = {(c["part"], c["token"]) for c in reask_claims(edition, out)}
        claims = [c for c in claims if (c["part"], c["token"]) not in reasked]
    return marks, claims


def pass_items(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    marks, claims = pass_claims(edition, out)
    return calls(marks, claims, context(edition, "full"), "u")


def reask_claims(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """§6's German box answers, asked again of the entry (D-196): for every mark §6 put to Opus
    where Opus named something other than the route's value, the route's value and the symbol Opus
    named, each a claim on the part, unless §6 already asked it of the entry. The Czech run: none."""
    folder = Path(out) / edition / "opus_pass"
    if edition not in GERMAN or not (folder / "items.json").exists() or not (folder / "answers_raw.csv").exists():
        return []
    items = {i["id"]: i for i in json.loads((folder / "items.json").read_text("utf-8"))}
    final = {m["mark"]: m["final"] for m in _read(rf.out_dir(edition, "full") / "ledger" / f"marks_{edition}_full.csv")}
    vocabulary, held = set(rf._english(edition)), held_sets(edition, "full")
    answered, asked = {}, set()
    for a in _read(folder / "answers_raw.csv"):
        item = items.get(a["item"])
        q = next((q for q in (item or {}).get("questions", []) if str(q["n"]) == str(a["question"])), None)
        if q is None:
            continue
        part = tuple(map(str, item["unit"]))
        if q["kind"] == "mark":
            answered[led.box_mark(q["image"], ",".join(map(str, q["box"])))] = (part, a["answer"])
        else:
            asked.add((part, q["token"]))
    out_, seen = [], set()
    for mark, (part, answer) in sorted(answered.items()):
        route = final.get(mark, "")
        if answer == route:
            continue
        for token in (route, answer):
            key = (part, token)
            if not token.startswith("[") or token not in vocabulary or key in asked or key in seen:
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            out_.append({"mark": mark, "part": part, "token": token,
                         "route": token if token in held.get(part, set()) else led.ABSENT, "gold": ""})
    return out_


def reask_items(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    return calls([], reask_claims(edition, out), context(edition, "full"), "e")


def editions_claims(out: Path = OUT, n_parts: int = EDITIONS_PARTS, seed: int = SEED,
                    judge: "ec.Judge | None" = None) -> list[dict]:
    """The Czech-volume test's claims: every claim of :data:`EDITIONS_PARTS` German Böhmen parts drawn
    from the pass and the re-ask, on municipalities paired one to one with the Czech volume, never on a
    sealed, reserve or gold page."""
    ed = ec.EDITION
    pool = pass_claims(ed, out)[1] + reask_claims(ed, out)
    judge = judge or ec.Judge(out)
    held_out = rf.sealed_pages(ed) | rf.in_sample_pages(ed)
    parts = sorted({c["part"] for c in pool if c["part"][0] not in held_out
                    and judge.partner(*c["part"][:3]) is not ec.UNPAIRED})
    chosen = set(random.Random(seed).sample(parts, min(n_parts, len(parts))))
    return [c for c in pool if c["part"] in chosen]


def editions_items(out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    return calls([], editions_claims(out), context(ec.EDITION, "full"), "x")


def followup_claims(edition: str, pages: str, stage: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """The claims behind the boxes ``stage`` found empty (:data:`EMPTY`): every symbol a reader named
    at such a box, as a claim on its part, unless the stage already asked it of the entry."""
    got = answers(edition, stage, out)
    asked = {k for k in got if isinstance(k, tuple)}
    marks, _claims = group(edition, pages)
    claims, seen = [], set()
    for m in marks:
        if got.get(m["mark"]) not in EMPTY:
            continue
        for token in m["placed"]:
            key = (m["part"], token)
            if key in asked or key in seen:
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            claims.append({"mark": m["mark"], "part": m["part"], "token": token,
                           "route": token if m["route"] == token else led.ABSENT, "gold": ""})
    return claims


def followup_items(edition: str, pages: str, stage: str, prefix: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """One call per part: its claims behind empty boxes, *is it printed for this part?*, with the
    part's sections in view (as the route asks a German part, D-118c)."""
    return calls([], followup_claims(edition, pages, stage, out), context(edition, pages), prefix)

def answers(edition: str, stage: str, out: Path = OUT) -> dict:
    """``{mark: answer}`` for outlined marks and ``{(part, token): answer}`` for entry claims."""
    folder = Path(out) / edition / stage
    if not (folder / "items.json").exists() or not (folder / "answers_raw.csv").exists():
        return {}
    items = {i["id"]: i for i in json.loads((folder / "items.json").read_text("utf-8"))}
    got = {}
    for a in _read(folder / "answers_raw.csv"):
        item = items.get(a["item"])
        q = next((q for q in (item or {}).get("questions", []) if str(q["n"]) == str(a["question"])), None)
        if q is None:
            continue
        if q["kind"] == "mark":
            got[led.box_mark(q["image"], ",".join(map(str, q["box"])))] = a["answer"]
        else:
            got[(tuple(item["unit"]), q["token"])] = a["answer"]
    return got


def opus_value(answer: str, token: str = "") -> str:
    """An answer on the ledger's scale: a mark's *none of these* is no symbol; an entry claim's
    *printed* is its token, *not printed* no symbol. *Cannot tell* stays."""
    if answer == tb.NONE:
        return led.ABSENT
    if token:
        return token if answer == tb.PRINTED else (led.ABSENT if answer == tb.ABSENT else answer)
    return answer


def _judged(r: dict) -> dict:
    """A row as ``{group, right, route_right, changed, empty}``. A row with ``truth`` compares
    values; a gold box row comes already judged (:func:`gold_rows`)."""
    if "truth" not in r:
        return r
    a = r["answer"]
    return {"group": r["group"], "right": a == r["truth"], "route_right": r["truth"] == r["route"],
            "changed": a != tb.UNSURE and a != r["route"], "empty": False}


def score(rows: list[dict]) -> list[dict]:
    """Per group, D-190's bars: ``rows`` — ``{group, truth, route, answer}`` with answer on the
    ledger's scale (*cannot tell* is a disagreement and changes nothing), or already judged
    ``{group, right, route_right, changed, empty}``. An *empty* row (a gold box Opus found empty,
    left to the follow-up) is counted apart and not in the share."""
    out = []
    groups = sorted({r["group"] for r in rows})
    rows = [_judged(r) for r in rows if r.get("answer", "") is not None]   # not answered: not asked
    for name in groups:
        mine = [r for r in rows if r["group"] == name]
        asked = [r for r in mine if not r.get("empty")]
        agree = sum(r["right"] for r in asked)
        confirmed = [r for r in asked if r["route_right"]]
        changed = sum(r["changed"] for r in confirmed)
        n = len(asked)
        low = op.wilson_low(agree, n)
        judged = n >= op.BAR["min_group"]
        out.append({"group": name, "n": n, "empty": len(mine) - n, "agree": agree, "share": round(agree / n, 3) if n else None,
                    "wilson_low": round(low, 3), "confirmed": len(confirmed), "changed": changed,
                    "change_share": round(changed / len(confirmed), 3) if confirmed else None,
                    "passes": (not judged) or (agree / n >= op.BAR["agree"] and low >= op.BAR["wilson_low"]
                                               and (not confirmed or changed / len(confirmed) <= op.BAR["change_max"])),
                    "judged": judged})
    return out


def gold_types(edition: str) -> dict:
    """``{part: place types}`` the gold sheets' parts lists print (section 2a), keyed as the route
    keys its types (``run_frozen.type_claims``). Calibration and purposive sheets only, never
    certification (``score_r5_german_notes.sheet_paths``)."""
    printed = defaultdict(set)
    if edition not in GOLD_SHEETS:
        return printed
    for path in sg.sheet_paths(GOLD_SHEETS[edition]):
        for p in rt.read_sheet(path, set())["parts"]:
            token = str(p.get("topographic") or "")
            if token.startswith("[") and token != "[?]":
                printed[(str(p["page"]), str(p["district"]), str(p["muni_seq"]),
                         str(p["settlement_seq"] or "0"))].add(token)
    return printed


def gold_parts(edition: str) -> dict:
    """``{part: tokens}`` the author's transcription prints: its offices (``run_frozen.gold_truth``)
    and, since 0.4.1, the place types of its parts lists (:func:`gold_types`). Without them every
    place-type question scored *not printed* (the 2026-11-09 run: the route itself right on 23 of
    39 Czech gold marks)."""
    printed = defaultdict(set)
    for page, district, muni, seq, token in rf.gold_truth(edition):
        printed[(page, district, muni, seq)].add(token)
    for part, tokens in gold_types(edition).items():
        printed[part] |= tokens
    return printed


def gold_read(edition: str) -> set:
    """``{(page, court)}`` the gold sheets read. A sheet may read one court of its page only
    (``read: court`` — 0478 reads Trautenau, not Marschendorf and Schatzlar above it), and a claim
    on a court it does not read has no truth: it is left out, not scored *not printed* (the
    2026-11-09 run scored 9 German Böhmen parts so)."""
    return ({(str(r["page"]), str(r["district"])) for r in rf.gold_rows(edition)}
            | {part[:2] for part in gold_types(edition)})


def gold_rows(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """The gold test against the author's transcription. The gold is transcribed by entry, not by
    box (a boxed mark's ``gold`` is always absent), so a box answer is judged on its part: right if
    the part prints the symbol Opus named there, a change if the route's own value was printed and
    Opus named another. *None of these* and *cannot tell* are left to the follow-up (``empty``)."""
    got = answers(edition, STAGE_GOLD, out)
    printed, read = gold_parts(edition), gold_read(edition)
    marks, claims = group(edition, "gold")
    marks = [m for m in marks if tuple(m["part"][:2]) in read]
    claims = [c for c in claims if tuple(c["part"][:2]) in read]
    rows = []
    for m in (marks if edition not in GERMAN else []):
        if m["mark"] not in got:
            continue
        answer, on_part = got[m["mark"]], printed.get(m["part"], set())
        rows.append({"group": f"{edition} gold, marks", "empty": answer in EMPTY, "right": answer in on_part,
                     "route_right": m["route"] in on_part,
                     "changed": answer not in EMPTY and answer != m["route"]})
    if edition in GERMAN:                         # D-196: the marks were asked as entry claims
        claims = as_entry_claims(marks, claims, edition, "gold")
    rows += [{"group": f"{edition} gold, entries",
              "truth": c["token"] if c["token"] in printed.get(c["part"], set()) else led.ABSENT,
              "route": c["route"] if c["route"] in (c["token"], led.ABSENT) else led.ABSENT,
              "answer": opus_value(got[(c["part"], c["token"])], c["token"]) if (c["part"], c["token"]) in got else None}
             for c in claims]
    return rows


def hand_rulings(folder: Path = HAND_RULINGS) -> dict:
    """``{id: value}`` from the page's saved rulings: a token, :data:`HAND_NONE` (no symbol) or
    :data:`HAND_UNSURE` (left out)."""
    got = {}
    for path in sorted(Path(folder).glob("*.json")):
        d = json.loads(path.read_text("utf-8"))
        if d.get("v") and d["v"] != HAND_UNSURE:
            got[d["id"]] = led.ABSENT if d["v"] == HAND_NONE else d["v"]
    return got


def hand_rows(out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """The hand test's marks against your rulings."""
    ruled = hand_rulings()
    got = {}
    for ed in {k["edition"] for k in hand_key()}:
        got.update(answers(ed, STAGE_HAND, out))
    return [{"group": f"{k['edition']} your rulings", "truth": ruled[k["id"]], "route": k["route"],
             "answer": opus_value(got[k["mark"]]) if k["mark"] in got else None}
            for k in hand_key() if k["id"] in ruled]


def write_score(stage: str, rows: list[dict], out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    result = score(rows)
    (Path(out) / f"{stage}_score.json").write_text(json.dumps(result, indent=1), encoding="utf-8")
    return result


def followup_gold_rows(edition: str, out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """The follow-up's gold test: each claim behind an empty gold box against the transcription."""
    got = answers(edition, STAGE_FOLLOW_GOLD, out)
    printed, read = gold_parts(edition), gold_read(edition)
    return [{"group": f"{edition} gold, behind empty boxes",
             "truth": c["token"] if c["token"] in printed.get(c["part"], set()) else led.ABSENT,
             "route": c["route"],
             "answer": opus_value(got[(c["part"], c["token"])], c["token"]) if (c["part"], c["token"]) in got else None}
            for c in followup_claims(edition, "gold", STAGE_GOLD, out) if tuple(c["part"][:2]) in read]


def editions_rows(out: Path = OUT) -> list[dict]:
    """The Czech-volume test: every entry question it sent, judged by what the paired Czech
    municipality prints (``editions_check.Judge``). Read off the calls sent, so the draw is the one
    asked. The Czech volume speaks of a municipality, so the route and the answer are taken to the
    municipality too (0.4.1): the route holds a symbol where any part of the German municipality
    does, and *not printed for this part* leaves it held where another part still holds it."""
    folder = Path(out) / ec.EDITION / STAGE_EDITIONS
    if not (folder / "items.json").exists():
        return []
    got, judge, held = answers(ec.EDITION, STAGE_EDITIONS, out), ec.Judge(out), held_sets(ec.EDITION, "full")
    types = {r["code"] for r in _read(ROOT / "reference" / "places" / "institution_abbreviations.csv")
             if r["category"] == "settlement"}
    parts_holding = defaultdict(set)                     # (page, district, muni, token) -> parts
    for p, codes in held.items():
        for code in codes:
            parts_holding[p[:3] + (code,)].add(p[3])
    rows = []
    for item in json.loads((folder / "items.json").read_text("utf-8")):
        part = tuple(map(str, item["unit"]))
        for q in item["questions"]:
            if q["kind"] != "token":
                continue
            token = q["token"]
            truth = judge.truth(part, token)
            if truth is ec.UNPAIRED:
                continue
            holding = parts_holding.get(part[:3] + (token,), set())
            answer = opus_value(got[(part, token)], token) if (part, token) in got else None
            if answer == led.ABSENT and holding - {part[3]}:
                answer = token                           # another part still holds it
            kind = "place types" if token in types else "institutions"
            rows.append({"group": f"de-bohemia against the Czech volume: {kind}", "truth": truth,
                         "route": token if holding else led.ABSENT, "answer": answer})
    return rows


def _scored_and_passed(stage: str, out: Path, edition: str | None = None) -> bool:
    """``stage`` scored and over the bars: every group, or with ``edition`` that edition's groups
    (a group is named for its edition), so one volume's test does not hold back another's. At least
    one group must be large enough to judge (``op.BAR["min_group"]``): D-190 lets a small group pass
    unjudged, which is right for a pair the author barely ruled, but a test with no judged group
    has measured nothing and opens no pass (the Czech gold, 2026-11-09: 39 marks, 9 changed)."""
    path = Path(out) / f"{stage}_score.json"
    if not path.exists():
        return False
    rows = [r for r in json.loads(path.read_text("utf-8")) if edition is None or r["group"].split()[0] == edition]
    return any(r["judged"] for r in rows) and all(r["passes"] for r in rows)


def followup_passed(out: Path = OUT) -> bool:
    """The follow-up's gold test scored and over the bars, a judged group among them."""
    return _scored_and_passed(STAGE_FOLLOW_GOLD, out)


def passed(out: Path = OUT, edition: str | None = None) -> bool:
    """The tests that gate ``edition``'s answers, scored and every judged group over the bars: the
    gold test, and for a German edition (or for none named) the Czech-volume test (D-196). The
    test on the author's 120 rulings is a diagnostic of the box and gates nothing since D-196."""
    german = edition is None or edition in GERMAN
    gold_of = edition if edition in GOLD_EDITIONS else ("de-bohemia" if german else None)  # Böhmen's gold speaks for the German volumes
    return (_scored_and_passed(STAGE_GOLD, out, gold_of)
            and (not german or _scored_and_passed(STAGE_EDITIONS, out)))

def send(edition: str, pages: str, stage: str, items: list[dict], spend: bool, out: Path = OUT,
         allow_other_machine: bool = False) -> dict:
    """``opus_pass.send`` for any run's pages: writes the calls, prices what is left, sends only
    with ``spend`` and only on the machine of record."""
    folder = aim(edition, pages, stage, out)
    blind = [i for i in items if i["view"] == "none"]
    items = [i for i in items if i["view"] != "none"]
    try:
        tb.write_items(items)
    except ValueError:
        if not (folder / "items.json").exists():
            raise
    done = tb._answered()
    in_flight = {cid for b in tb._batches() if not b.get("collected") for cid in b["ids"]}
    todo = [i for i in items if f"r1-{i['id']}" not in done | in_flight]
    low, high = op.price(todo)
    result = {"edition": edition, "pages": pages, "stage": stage, "calls": len(items), "no_image": len(blind),
              "questions": sum(len(i["questions"]) for i in items),
              "answered": sum(f"r1-{i['id']}" in done for i in items), "to_send": len(todo),
              "usd": (round(low, 2), round(high, 2)), "sent": None}
    if spend and todo:
        if op.bo.HOST != op.MACHINE_OF_RECORD and not allow_other_machine:
            raise RuntimeError(f"Opus is sent from {op.MACHINE_OF_RECORD} only (D-68): this is {op.bo.HOST}")
        result["sent"] = rf.opus_submit(todo, edition)
    (folder / "send.json").write_text(json.dumps(result, indent=1), encoding="utf-8")
    return result


def collect(edition: str, pages: str, stage: str, out: Path = OUT) -> bool | None:
    aim(edition, pages, stage, out)
    return tb.collect() if tb.BATCHES.exists() else None

    ap.add_argument("--stage", choices=("gold", "hand", "editions", "reask", "pass", "followup-gold", "followup"),
                    default="gold")
    args = ap.parse_args(argv)
    if hasattr(sys.stdout, "reconfigure"):

    out = Path(args.out)
    if args.stage == "gold":
        for ed in GOLD_EDITIONS:
            print(send(ed, "gold", STAGE_GOLD, gold_items(ed), spend=False, out=out))

        for ed in sorted({k["edition"] for k in hand_key()}):
            print(send(ed, "full", STAGE_HAND, hand_items(ed), spend=False, out=out))
    elif args.stage == "editions":
        print(send(ec.EDITION, "full", STAGE_EDITIONS, editions_items(out), spend=False, out=out))
    elif args.stage == "reask":
        for ed in GERMAN:
            print(send(ed, "full", STAGE_REASK, reask_items(ed, out), spend=False, out=out))
    elif args.stage == "pass":
        for ed in EDITIONS:
            print(send(ed, "full", STAGE_PASS, pass_items(ed, out), spend=False, out=out))
    elif args.stage == "followup-gold":
        for ed in GOLD_EDITIONS:
            print(send(ed, "gold", STAGE_FOLLOW_GOLD, followup_items(ed, "gold", STAGE_GOLD, "fg", out),
                       spend=False, out=out))
    else:
        for ed in EDITIONS:
            print(send(ed, "full", STAGE_FOLLOW, followup_items(ed, "full", STAGE_PASS, "f", out),
                       spend=False, out=out))
