How it works

The register the analysis reads (data/final/register_rechecked, D-200) has been corrected step by step, and every check so far reached only where a step changed something: §8b compares identical tables on the certification pages (D-198), §8a judges German Böhmen's changes against the Czech volume. What the register gets wrong where nothing looked — and whether that error is correlated with what the paper studies — is measured here, on entries no one has ruled on.

The frame: every municipality of the rechecked register anchored to a 1910 settlement (settlement_id: the ones the analysis can reach) in the Czech volume (Bohemia's source, 7,515 municipalities against German Böhmen's 82, D-198), Mähren and Schlesien — never one printed on a sealed, reserve, calibration or gold page (run_frozen.sealed_pages | in_sample_pages), never one the author has ruled on or seen (an author change, the blind sample, the look-alike families, 15b's validation marks, the 120 Unasked Marks).

The strata (frame, allocate), per volume:

  • §6d — A: the entry holds a claim of the group §6d was to ask about (D-194: the reader of record's reading kept without asking; in the Czech volume also opus settled nothing), which 15b found about half wrong on the route; B: the rest. A is oversampled to decide §6d.
  • language — Czech-, German- or (Schlesien's Teschen communes) Polish-majority by the register's table (lang_czech, lang_german, lang_polish; unknown without figures);
  • status — town (a town or market town, place_status on any of the entry's rows: with parts, the status sits on the namesake part) or other.

SIZES entries a volume and stratum, at least FLOOR in every language × status cell (all of a smaller cell), the rest in proportion to the cells' sizes (largest remainder); drawn at random (SEED) inside each cell. Every entry's inclusion probability n/N is in the key, so every rate is reweighted to its volume (Horvitz–Thompson).

The question (the page, blind): the entry's offices lines in a window of the whole section, and the volume's whole set of pictograms; mark every pictogram printed anywhere in this entry's offices lines (all its parts), or none, not in view, cannot tell. Nothing on the page depends on what the register holds, so the sample measures recall as well as precision. Counts are not asked; text codes (the cooperative letters, Notar …) are not in the sample.

The score (score), at the municipality level the analysis uses: the register's pictograms for the entry (register_institutions, all parts) against the author's. Per volume, stratum, language and status: entries exactly right, claims false and missed, and the weighted precision, recall and F1; their Korn–Graubard intervals (intervals, korn_graubard) and, per covariate, sensitivity, specificity and precision (covariates). Bars, fixed before the ruling (D-226):

  • §6d — in a volume the group rests if, on its A entries, the register is right on every symbol the group was in doubt about (the readers' symbols, the route's and their look-alikes) on at least 90 % of them, with a Wilson 95 % lower bound of at least 0.80; otherwise a targeted fix is planned for that volume.
  • correlated error — reported, not gated: weighted rates by language and by status, each difference with Newcombe's interval (newcombe).

The second look (reconcile_page): every symbol the blind ruling and the register disagree on is asked again, one card each, in the same window and without saying which side had it. The blind ruling is the primary result and carries the bars, as pre-registered (D-229, which reverses the amendment of D-226 that had made the adjudicated ruling the headline); the adjudicated ruling (reconciled) is a sensitivity analysis. Neither is a bound on the truth: an error the coder and the register share counts as right in both.

Not merged: the rulings are a measurement of the register as analysed, so they are not laid over it; the errors found are listed (errors) for the author to rule on separately.

def frame(edition: str, recheck: Path = RECHECK, register: Path = REGISTER) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Every eligible municipality of one volume with its stratum, language, status, the register's
    pictograms, the §6d symbols in doubt and where the page prints it."""
    places = pd.read_parquet(Path(register) / "register_places.parquet")
    inst = pd.read_parquet(Path(register) / "register_institutions.parquet")
    # Anchored municipalities only: one with no settlement_id (an unmatched name, a Jewish community
    # listed as its own municipality …) never reaches the persons or the census, so it is not the
    # register as analysed (523 in the three volumes on 2026-11-11).
    mun = places[(places["edition"] == edition) & (places["place_level"] == "municipality") & places["muni_seq"].notna()
                 & places["settlement_id"].notna()].copy()
    mun["court_key"] = mun["court_key"].astype(str)
    mun["muni_seq"] = mun["muni_seq"].astype(int).astype(str)
    pics = inst[(inst["edition"] == edition) & (inst["source"] == "pictogram")]
    held = defaultdict(set)
    boxes = defaultdict(list)
    for r in pics.itertuples():
        key = (str(r.court_key), str(int(r.muni_seq)))
        held[key].add(r.mark)
        boxes[key] += [m for m in str(r.marks or "").split(" | ") if "_offices_" in m.split("#")[0] and ".png#" in m]
    doubt = doubt_by_entry(edition, recheck)
    _held, where, _usual = printed_holdings(edition, recheck)
    label, labels = {}, defaultdict(set)                 # (court, register muni) -> (page, district) the page prints
    for (page, district, _muni, _seq), (court, muni) in sorted(where.items()):
        label.setdefault((court, muni), (page, district))
        labels[(court, muni)].add((page, district))      # every page and label the entry's parts are printed under
    towns = {(str(r.court_key), str(int(r.muni_seq))) for r in places[(places["edition"] == edition)
             & places["place_status"].isin(TOWN) & places["muni_seq"].notna()].itertuples()}
    court_label = defaultdict(dict)                      # court -> {page: the run's label}: an entry the tables hold nothing for
    for c in _read(rf.out_dir(edition, "full") / "courts.csv"):
        if c.get("court_id"):
            court_label[str(c["court_id"])].setdefault(str(c["page"]).zfill(4), c["district"])
    skip_pages = rf.sealed_pages(edition) | rf.in_sample_pages(edition)
    seen_printed, seen_keyed, seen_number = seen_entries(edition, recheck)
    checked = hand_checked(edition)
    rows = []
    for r in mun.itertuples():
        key = (r.court_key, r.muni_seq)
        pages = [p for p in str(r.pages or "").replace(",", ";").replace(" ", ";").split(";") if p]
        page, district = label.get(key, ("", ""))
        if not page:
            page = next((p for p in pages if p in court_label.get(r.court_key, {})), pages[0] if pages else "")
            district = court_label.get(r.court_key, {}).get(page, "")
        printed = printed_number(r.muni_seq)
        # Seen on any page it is printed on, under any label (Reichwaldau 5: ruled on 0024, labelled 0022).
        printed_as = labels.get(key, set()) | {(page, district)} | {
            (p, court_label.get(r.court_key, {}).get(p, "")) for p in pages}
        if (set(pages) & skip_pages or page in skip_pages or key in seen_keyed or (r.court_key, printed) in seen_number
                or any((p, d, printed) in seen_printed for p, d in printed_as)):
            continue
        rows.append({"edition": edition, "court_key": r.court_key, "muni_seq": r.muni_seq, "court_cs": r.court_cs,
                     "printed_name": r.printed_name, "page": page, "pages": pages or [page], "district": district,
                     "printed_muni": printed,
                     "stratum": "A" if doubt.get(key) else "B", "language": language(r.lang_czech, r.lang_german, r.lang_polish),
                     # a town's status sits on its namesake part when it has parts (Blatná, Česká Lípa)
                     "status": "town" if key in towns else "other", "held": sorted(held.get(key, set())),
                     "doubt": sorted(doubt.get(key, set())), "boxes": boxes.get(key, []),
                     "hand_checked": (page, district, printed) in checked})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

def allocate(cells: dict, n: int, floor: int = FLOOR) -> dict:
    """``{cell: size}`` -> ``{cell: drawn}``: at least ``floor`` in every cell (all of a smaller one),
    the rest in proportion to the cells' sizes, by largest remainder; never more than a cell holds."""
    take = {c: min(floor, size) for c, size in cells.items()}
    left = n - sum(take.values())
    while left > 0:
        room = {c: cells[c] - take[c] for c in cells if cells[c] > take[c]}
        if not room:
            break
        total = sum(room.values())
        quota = {c: left * r / total for c, r in room.items()}
        add = {c: min(room[c], int(q)) for c, q in quota.items()}
        if not sum(add.values()):                     # fewer left than cells: largest remainders, one each
            for c in sorted(quota, key=lambda c: (-(quota[c] - int(quota[c])), c))[:left]:
                add[c] = 1
        for c, a in add.items():
            take[c] += a
        left = n - sum(take.values())
    return take

    """token → rank in the order offices lines print, from where the rechecked table's marks stand: inside
    each entry the marks are put in reading order (line, then left to right), every pair of tokens is
    counted, and a token ranks by how many others it precedes on most entries (Copeland). A volume-wide

    lines = defaultdict(list)
    for r in _read(Path(recheck) / edition / "final_inventory_rechecked.csv"):
        box = _mark_box(r.get("marks", ""))

        marks.sort(key=lambda m: (round(m[2] / height), m[1]))

    return {t: k for k, t in enumerate(ordered)}

def ratio(rows: list[tuple]) -> tuple[float, float]:
    """Weighted ratio Σwy / Σwx over ``(weight, y, x, cell, fraction)`` and its linearised standard error
    for a stratified draw without replacement: within each cell (volume × stratum × language × status)
    the variance of the residuals ``w (y − R x)``, times ``n/(n − 1)`` and the finite-population
    correction ``1 − n/N`` (``fraction`` = the cell's sampling fraction); a cell of one adds nothing."""
    sx = sum(w * x for w, _y, x, *_r in rows)
    if not sx:
        return float("nan"), float("nan")
    r = sum(w * y for w, y, _x, *_r in rows) / sx
    cells = defaultdict(list)
    for w, y, x, cell, fraction in rows:
        cells[cell].append((w * (y - r * x), fraction))
    var = 0.0
    for members in cells.values():
        n = len(members)
        if n < 2:
            continue
        z = [v for v, _f in members]
        mean = sum(z) / n
        var += (1 - members[0][1]) * n / (n - 1) * sum((v - mean) ** 2 for v in z)
    return r, math.sqrt(var) / sx

def korn_graubard(point: float, variance: float, units: float, deff_weights: float) -> list:
    """Korn & Graubard (1998): the Clopper–Pearson interval on the effective sample size p(1 − p) / variance,
    capped at ``units``, the raw count of what the rate is a share of (claims, printed symbols, entries).
    Where that size is undefined — no error seen, or no hit, so the variance is zero — the effective size is
    ``units / deff_weights`` (Kish's design effect of the weights over those units), so a rate of 100 % still
    gets an interval below it."""
    from scipy.stats import beta
    if point is None or not units:
        return [None, None]
    if 0 < point < 1 and variance > 0:
        n = min(units, point * (1 - point) / variance)
    else:
        n = units / max(deff_weights, 1.0)
    x = point * n
    lo = 0.0 if x <= 0 else float(beta.ppf(0.025, x, n - x + 1))
    hi = 1.0 if x >= n else float(beta.ppf(0.975, x + 1, n - x))
    return [round(lo, 4), round(hi, 4)]

    ``(point, [lo, hi])`` each; the difference is a − b."""

    """One row per volume for the blind ruling (primary) and one for the adjudicated ruling (a sensitivity
    analysis): entries ruled; entries exactly right, precision, recall and F1, weighted, with 95 % intervals;

    rows = []
    for ed in EDITIONS:
        for name, block, band in (("Blind", result["blind"], result["intervals"]["blind"]),
                                  ("Adjudicated", result["adjudicated"], result["intervals"]["adjudicated"])):

            if not a["ruled"]:
                continue
            i = band[ed]["all"]

                         "Exact": _cell(i["exact"], 2), "Precision": _cell(i["precision"], 2),
                         "Recall": _cell(i["recall"], 2), "F1": _cell(i["f1"], 2),

    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

def language(czech, german, polish=None) -> str:
    """``cs``, ``de`` or ``pl`` by the table's largest language (Schlesien's Teschen communes are Polish);
    ``unknown`` without figures or on a tie."""
    counts = {k: (0.0 if v is None or pd.isna(v) else float(v)) for k, v in (("cs", czech), ("de", german), ("pl", polish))}
    top = max(counts.values())
    leaders = [k for k, v in counts.items() if v == top]
    return "unknown" if top == 0 or len(leaders) > 1 else leaders[0]

def frame(edition: str, recheck: Path = RECHECK, register: Path = REGISTER) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Every eligible municipality of one volume with its stratum, language, status, the register's
    pictograms, the §6d symbols in doubt and where the page prints it."""
    places = pd.read_parquet(Path(register) / "register_places.parquet")
    inst = pd.read_parquet(Path(register) / "register_institutions.parquet")
    # Anchored municipalities only: one with no settlement_id (an unmatched name, a Jewish community
    # listed as its own municipality …) never reaches the persons or the census, so it is not the
    # register as analysed (523 in the three volumes on 2026-11-11).
    mun = places[(places["edition"] == edition) & (places["place_level"] == "municipality") & places["muni_seq"].notna()
                 & places["settlement_id"].notna()].copy()
    mun["court_key"] = mun["court_key"].astype(str)
    mun["muni_seq"] = mun["muni_seq"].astype(int).astype(str)
    pics = inst[(inst["edition"] == edition) & (inst["source"] == "pictogram")]
    held = defaultdict(set)
    boxes = defaultdict(list)
    for r in pics.itertuples():
        key = (str(r.court_key), str(int(r.muni_seq)))
        held[key].add(r.mark)
        boxes[key] += [m for m in str(r.marks or "").split(" | ") if "_offices_" in m.split("#")[0] and ".png#" in m]
    doubt = doubt_by_entry(edition, recheck)
    _held, where, _usual = printed_holdings(edition, recheck)
    label, labels = {}, defaultdict(set)                 # (court, register muni) -> (page, district) the page prints
    for (page, district, _muni, _seq), (court, muni) in sorted(where.items()):
        label.setdefault((court, muni), (page, district))
        labels[(court, muni)].add((page, district))      # every page and label the entry's parts are printed under
    towns = {(str(r.court_key), str(int(r.muni_seq))) for r in places[(places["edition"] == edition)
             & places["place_status"].isin(TOWN) & places["muni_seq"].notna()].itertuples()}
    court_label = defaultdict(dict)                      # court -> {page: the run's label}: an entry the tables hold nothing for
    for c in _read(rf.out_dir(edition, "full") / "courts.csv"):
        if c.get("court_id"):
            court_label[str(c["court_id"])].setdefault(str(c["page"]).zfill(4), c["district"])
    skip_pages = rf.sealed_pages(edition) | rf.in_sample_pages(edition)
    seen_printed, seen_keyed, seen_number = seen_entries(edition, recheck)
    checked = hand_checked(edition)
    rows = []
    for r in mun.itertuples():
        key = (r.court_key, r.muni_seq)
        pages = [p for p in str(r.pages or "").replace(",", ";").replace(" ", ";").split(";") if p]
        page, district = label.get(key, ("", ""))
        if not page:
            page = next((p for p in pages if p in court_label.get(r.court_key, {})), pages[0] if pages else "")
            district = court_label.get(r.court_key, {}).get(page, "")
        printed = printed_number(r.muni_seq)
        # Seen on any page it is printed on, under any label (Reichwaldau 5: ruled on 0024, labelled 0022).
        printed_as = labels.get(key, set()) | {(page, district)} | {
            (p, court_label.get(r.court_key, {}).get(p, "")) for p in pages}
        if (set(pages) & skip_pages or page in skip_pages or key in seen_keyed or (r.court_key, printed) in seen_number
                or any((p, d, printed) in seen_printed for p, d in printed_as)):
            continue
        rows.append({"edition": edition, "court_key": r.court_key, "muni_seq": r.muni_seq, "court_cs": r.court_cs,
                     "printed_name": r.printed_name, "page": page, "pages": pages or [page], "district": district,
                     "printed_muni": printed,
                     "stratum": "A" if doubt.get(key) else "B", "language": language(r.lang_czech, r.lang_german, r.lang_polish),
                     # a town's status sits on its namesake part when it has parts (Blatná, Česká Lípa)
                     "status": "town" if key in towns else "other", "held": sorted(held.get(key, set())),
                     "doubt": sorted(doubt.get(key, set())), "boxes": boxes.get(key, []),
                     "hand_checked": (page, district, printed) in checked})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

def allocate(cells: dict, n: int, floor: int = FLOOR) -> dict:
    """``{cell: size}`` -> ``{cell: drawn}``: at least ``floor`` in every cell (all of a smaller one),
    the rest in proportion to the cells' sizes, by largest remainder; never more than a cell holds."""
    take = {c: min(floor, size) for c, size in cells.items()}
    left = n - sum(take.values())
    while left > 0:
        room = {c: cells[c] - take[c] for c in cells if cells[c] > take[c]}
        if not room:
            break
        total = sum(room.values())
        quota = {c: left * r / total for c, r in room.items()}
        add = {c: min(room[c], int(q)) for c, q in quota.items()}
        if not sum(add.values()):                     # fewer left than cells: largest remainders, one each
            for c in sorted(quota, key=lambda c: (-(quota[c] - int(quota[c])), c))[:left]:
                add[c] = 1
        for c, a in add.items():
            take[c] += a
        left = n - sum(take.values())
    return take


def draw(edition: str, recheck: Path = RECHECK, seed: int = SEED, sizes: dict = SIZES) -> list[dict]:
    """The volume's entries, drawn per stratum and language × status cell, each with its inclusion probability."""
    f = frame(edition, recheck)
    out = []
    for stratum in ("A", "B"):
        s = f[f["stratum"] == stratum]
        cells = s.groupby(["language", "status"]).size().to_dict()
        plan = allocate(cells, min(sizes[edition][stratum], len(s)))
        for cell in sorted(plan):
            members = s[(s["language"] == cell[0]) & (s["status"] == cell[1])].sort_values(["court_key", "muni_seq"],
                                                                                          key=lambda c: c.astype(int))
            rng = random.Random(f"{seed}-{edition}-{stratum}-{cell[0]}-{cell[1]}")
            picks = sorted(rng.sample(range(len(members)), plan[cell]))
            for k in picks:
                r = members.iloc[k].to_dict()
                p = plan[cell] / len(members)
                out.append({**r, "cell_size": len(members), "cell_drawn": plan[cell], "inclusion": round(p, 6),
                            "weight": round(1 / p, 6), "seed": seed})
    return out

def _same_court(edition: str, page: str, court: str) -> set:
    """Every label the run's ``courts.csv`` gives ``court`` on ``page`` (Polná's offices filed as *rolna*;
    Třešť, a post-1910 court, as *triesch*)."""
    return {c["district"] for c in _read(rf.out_dir(edition, "full") / "courts.csv")
            if str(c["page"]).zfill(4) == page and court and str(c.get("court_id", "")) == court}


def _fallback(edition: str, page: str, district: str, muni: str, court: str = "") -> tuple[str, bool]:
    """``(mark, own)``: the boxed offices mark of **another** entry nearest the entry's number on its page,
    under the entry's label, else under any label the run gives the same court there, else anywhere on
    the page; the entry's own mark only where the page has no other. Never the own mark by preference:
    whether a reader put a box on an entry depends on what was read there, and the window must not."""
    boxed = [m for m in _marks(edition) if m["page"] == page and "_offices_" in m["mark"].split("#")[0]
             and ".png#" in m["mark"] and str(m["muni"]).isdigit()]
    same = _same_court(edition, page, court)
    for rows in ([m for m in boxed if m["district"] == district], [m for m in boxed if m["district"] in same], boxed):
        if rows:
            rows.sort(key=lambda m: (int(m["muni"]) == int(muni), abs(int(m["muni"]) - int(muni)), int(m["muni"])))
            return rows[0]["mark"], int(rows[0]["muni"]) == int(muni)
    # No offices section on the page: the offices list sits inside another section image, as §4 found
    # (D-192's blind regions, ``offices_reread.regions``: Třebíč's on Mähren 0133 under its parts heading).
    from utils.recheck import offices_reread as orr
    for r in orr.regions(edition):
        if r["section"].startswith(f"{page}_"):
            y = int(float(r["y_from"] or 0))
            return f"{r['section']}.png#0,{y},1,{y + 1}", False
    return "", False


def more_sections(edition: str, pages: list[str], labels: set, shown: str) -> list[str]:
    """The court's other offices section images on the entry's pages, beside the window's own: an entry
    whose lines run on into the next section or page (8 in the first draw) is seen whole."""

            if path.name != shown and any(path.stem.startswith(f"{page}_{l}_offices_") for l in labels):
                out.append(path.name)
    return out

# The palette's groups, in the order an offices line prints them; a token in none of them is *Other*.
# Layout only: the page shows every pictogram on every card whatever its group (0.2.0, for ruling speed).
PALETTE_GROUPS = (
    ("Offices", {"[cadastre-office]", "[customs]", "[customs-exp]", "[revenue-admin]", "[revenue-technical]",
                 "[revenue-guard]", "[gauging]", "[cask-gauging]", "[gendarme]", "[canteen]", "[transport-station]"}),
    ("Churches", {"[jewish]", "[monastery]"}),          # + every rc-/ev- token (palette_group)
    ("Schools", {"[school]", "[school-exp]", "[kindergarten]", "[creche]"}),
    ("Health and welfare", {"[poorhouse]", "[hospital]", "[sanatorium]", "[doctor]", "[pharmacy]", "[midwife]",
                            "[vet]", "[farrier]", "[knacker]"}),
    ("Fire brigade and money", {"[fire-brigade]", "[savings-bank]", "[cooperative]"}),
    ("Post and transport", {"[post]", "[post-agency]", "[telegraph]", "[telephone]", "[rail-station]", "[rail-stop]",
                            "[steamer]", "[port]"}),
)
OTHER_GROUP = "Other"


def palette_group(token: str) -> str:
    if token.startswith(("[rc-", "[ev-")):
        return "Churches"
    return next((name for name, members in PALETTE_GROUPS if token in members), OTHER_GROUP)


def _mark_box(marks: str):
    """The first mark's image and centre (x, y) and height, or None."""
    first = (marks or "").split("|")[0].strip()
    if "#" not in first:
        return None
    img, box = first.split("#", 1)
    try:
        x0, y0, x1, y1 = (int(v) for v in box.split(","))
    except ValueError:
        return None
    return img, (x0 + x1) / 2, (y0 + y1) / 2, y1 - y0


def print_order(edition: str, recheck: Path = RECHECK) -> dict:
    """token → rank in the order offices lines print, from where the rechecked table's marks stand: inside
    each entry the marks are put in reading order (line, then left to right), every pair of tokens is
    counted, and a token ranks by how many others it precedes on most entries (Copeland). A volume-wide
    order, the same for every card, so it says nothing about any entry; it only lays out the palette."""
    lines = defaultdict(list)
    for r in _read(Path(recheck) / edition / "final_inventory_rechecked.csv"):
        box = _mark_box(r.get("marks", ""))
        if r["code"].startswith("[") and box:
            lines[(r["entry"], box[0])].append((r["code"], box[1], box[2], box[3]))
    before = defaultdict(int)
    seen = set()
    for marks in lines.values():
        if len(marks) < 2:
            continue
        height = sorted(m[3] for m in marks)[len(marks) // 2] or 60
        marks.sort(key=lambda m: (round(m[2] / height), m[1]))
        for i, (a, *_) in enumerate(marks):
            seen.add(a)
            for b, *_ in marks[i + 1:]:
                if a != b:
                    before[(a, b)] += 1
    wins = {a: sum(before[(a, b)] > before[(b, a)] for b in seen if b != a) for a in seen}
    ordered = sorted(seen, key=lambda a: (-wins[a], a))
    return {t: k for k, t in enumerate(ordered)}

def ruled_set(answer: dict | None) -> set | None:
    """The pictograms the author marked; ``None`` where the card sets nothing."""
    value = list((answer or {}).get("value") or [])
    if not value or set(value) & NOT_A_RULING:
        return None
    return set() if value == [NONE] else set(value) - {NONE}


def wilson(k: int, n: int, z: float = 1.96) -> tuple[float, float]:
    if n == 0:
        return float("nan"), float("nan")
    p = k / n
    centre, half = p + z * z / (2 * n), z * math.sqrt(p * (1 - p) / n + z * z / (4 * n * n))
    return max(0.0, (centre - half) / (1 + z * z / n)), min(1.0, (centre + half) / (1 + z * z / n))


def ratio(rows: list[tuple]) -> tuple[float, float]:
    """Weighted ratio Σwy / Σwx over ``(weight, y, x, cell, fraction)`` and its linearised standard error
    for a stratified draw without replacement: within each cell (volume × stratum × language × status)
    the variance of the residuals ``w (y − R x)``, times ``n/(n − 1)`` and the finite-population
    correction ``1 − n/N`` (``fraction`` = the cell's sampling fraction); a cell of one adds nothing."""
    sx = sum(w * x for w, _y, x, *_r in rows)
    if not sx:
        return float("nan"), float("nan")
    r = sum(w * y for w, y, _x, *_r in rows) / sx
    cells = defaultdict(list)
    for w, y, x, cell, fraction in rows:
        cells[cell].append((w * (y - r * x), fraction))
    var = 0.0
    for members in cells.values():
        n = len(members)
        if n < 2:
            continue
        z = [v for v, _f in members]
        mean = sum(z) / n
        var += (1 - members[0][1]) * n / (n - 1) * sum((v - mean) ** 2 for v in z)
    return r, math.sqrt(var) / sx


def _judged(key: list[dict], answers: dict) -> list[tuple[dict, set]]:
    out = []
    for r in key:
        s = ruled_set(answers.get(r["id"]))
        if s is not None:
            out.append((r, s))
    return out


def measures(judged: list[tuple[dict, set]]) -> dict:
    """Entries exactly right, false and missed claims, weighted exactness, precision, recall and F1 (their
    intervals are :func:`intervals`'; the linearised SEs with the finite-population correction are no longer
    reported, D-229)."""
    right = sum(set(r["held"]) == s for r, s in judged)
    false = sum(len(set(r["held"]) - s) for r, s in judged)
    missed = sum(len(s - set(r["held"])) for r, s in judged)
    def cell(r):
        return (r["edition"], r["stratum"], r["language"], r["status"])
    precision = ratio([(r["weight"], len(set(r["held"]) & s), len(r["held"]), cell(r), r["inclusion"]) for r, s in judged])
    recall = ratio([(r["weight"], len(set(r["held"]) & s), len(s), cell(r), r["inclusion"]) for r, s in judged])
    exact = ratio([(r["weight"], float(set(r["held"]) == s), 1.0, cell(r), r["inclusion"]) for r, s in judged])
    # F1 = 2·TP / (2·TP + FP + FN), a ratio of entry totals like the others
    f1 = ratio([(r["weight"], 2 * len(set(r["held"]) & s), len(set(r["held"])) + len(s), cell(r), r["inclusion"]) for r, s in judged])
    rd = lambda v: None if v is None or math.isnan(v) else round(v, 4)      # noqa: E731
    return {"ruled": len(judged), "exact": right, "false_claims": false, "missed_claims": missed,
            "w_exact": rd(exact[0]), "w_precision": rd(precision[0]), "w_recall": rd(recall[0]), "w_f1": rd(f1[0])}


def score(key: list[dict], answers: dict) -> dict:
    """Per volume: overall and by stratum, language and status; the §6d verdict on the *A* entries."""
    out = {}
    for ed in EDITIONS:
        rows = _judged([r for r in key if r["edition"] == ed], answers)
        block = {"all": measures(rows)}
        for field in ("stratum", "language", "status", "hand_checked"):
            for value in sorted({r[field] for r, _s in rows}):
                block[f"{field}={value}"] = measures([(r, s) for r, s in rows if r[field] == value])
        # The bar's denominator is every A entry answered at all: "not in view" and "cannot tell" count as
        # not right, so an entry nobody can read does not loosen a bar fixed at 55 of 60 / 33 of 35.
        answered = [r for r in key if r["edition"] == ed and r["stratum"] == "A" and (answers.get(r["id"]) or {}).get("value")]
        a = [(r, s) for r, s in rows if r["stratum"] == "A"]
        ok = sum(set(r["held"]) & set(r["doubt"]) == s & set(r["doubt"]) for r, s in a)
        lower, upper = wilson(ok, len(answered))
        share = ok / len(answered) if answered else float("nan")
        a = answered
        block["s6d"] = {"ruled": len(a), "right_on_doubt": ok, "share": None if not a else round(share, 3),
                        "wilson_lower": None if not a else round(lower, 3),
                        "verdict": ("no entries ruled" if not a else
                                    "rests" if share >= REST_SHARE and lower >= REST_LOWER else "fix planned")}
        out[ed] = block
    return out

def disputes(key: list[dict], answers: dict) -> list[dict]:
    """Every (entry, symbol) where the register and the blind ruling differ, both ways: ``side`` is
    ``register`` (the register holds it, the ruling did not mark it) or ``ruling``. The side stays in
    ``disputes.csv``; the page asks only whether the symbol is printed."""
    out = []
    for r, s in _judged(key, answers):

        for token, side in [(t, "register") for t in sorted(held - s)] + [(t, "ruling") for t in sorted(s - held)]:
            out.append({"id": f"{r['id']}~{token.strip('[]')}", "entry": r["id"], "edition": r["edition"],
                        "token": token, "side": side, "stratum": r["stratum"],
                        "in_doubt": token in set(r["doubt"])})
    return out

def reconciled(key: list[dict], answers: dict, second: dict) -> dict:
    """The blind rulings with each disputed symbol set by the second look: *printed* adds it, *absent*
    takes it out, *cannot tell* or no answer leaves the blind ruling as it was."""
    out = {i: dict(a) for i, a in answers.items()}
    for d in disputes(key, answers):
        verdict = str((second.get(d["id"]) or {}).get("value") or "")
        if verdict not in ("printed", "absent"):
            continue
        marked = ruled_set(out[d["entry"]]) or set()
        marked = marked | {d["token"]} if verdict == "printed" else marked - {d["token"]}
        out[d["entry"]] = {**out[d["entry"]], "value": sorted(marked) or [NONE]}
    return out


def second_look(key: list[dict], answers: dict, second: dict) -> dict:
    """How the disputes came out: per side (the register's claim, the ruling's mark) and for each symbol,
    which side the second look sided with."""
    tally = defaultdict(lambda: defaultdict(int))
    for d in disputes(key, answers):
        verdict = str((second.get(d["id"]) or {}).get("value") or "unanswered")
        sided = {"printed": d["side"], "absent": "ruling" if d["side"] == "register" else "register"}.get(verdict, verdict)
        tally[f"side={d['side']}"][f"sided_with_{sided}"] += 1
        tally[d["token"]][f"sided_with_{sided}"] += 1
    return {k: dict(v) for k, v in tally.items()}


def second_look_status(key: list[dict], answers: dict, second: dict) -> dict:
    """Per volume: ``complete`` when every disputed symbol has a second-look answer, else how many are open
    (an open one keeps the blind ruling, so the adjudicated score equals the blind one until the look is done)."""
    out = {}
    for ed in EDITIONS:
        found = [d for d in disputes(key, answers) if d["edition"] == ed]
        open_ = sum(not (second.get(d["id"]) or {}).get("value") for d in found)
        ruled = sum(bool(ruled_set(answers.get(r["id"])) is not None) for r in key if r["edition"] == ed)
        out[ed] = ("not ruled" if not ruled else "complete" if not open_ else f"{open_} of {len(found)} open")
    return out

def _entry_rows(key: list[dict], answers: dict) -> list[dict]:
    """One row per ruled entry: its design cell and weight, and the counts every rate is built from."""
    rows = []
    for r, s in _judged(key, answers):
        held = set(r["held"])
        rows.append({"cell": (r["edition"], r["stratum"], r["language"], r["status"]), "w": float(r["weight"]),
                     "edition": r["edition"], "group": "cz" if r["edition"] == "cz" else "de",
                     "stratum": r["stratum"], "language": r["language"], "status": r["status"],
                     "held_set": held, "marked_set": s, "tp": len(held & s), "held": len(held),
                     "marked": len(s), "exact": float(held == s)})
    return rows


def korn_graubard(point: float, variance: float, units: float, deff_weights: float) -> list:
    """Korn & Graubard (1998): the Clopper–Pearson interval on the effective sample size p(1 − p) / variance,
    capped at ``units``, the raw count of what the rate is a share of (claims, printed symbols, entries).
    Where that size is undefined — no error seen, or no hit, so the variance is zero — the effective size is
    ``units / deff_weights`` (Kish's design effect of the weights over those units), so a rate of 100 % still
    gets an interval below it."""
    from scipy.stats import beta
    if point is None or not units:
        return [None, None]
    if 0 < point < 1 and variance > 0:
        n = min(units, point * (1 - point) / variance)
    else:
        n = units / max(deff_weights, 1.0)
    x = point * n
    lo = 0.0 if x <= 0 else float(beta.ppf(0.025, x, n - x + 1))
    hi = 1.0 if x >= n else float(beta.ppf(0.975, x + 1, n - x))
    return [round(lo, 4), round(hi, 4)]


def newcombe(a: tuple, b: tuple) -> list:
    """Newcombe's (1998) interval for the difference of two independent rates from their own intervals:
    ``(point, [lo, hi])`` each; the difference is a − b."""
    import math as _m
    (p1, (l1, u1)), (p2, (l2, u2)) = a, b
    d = p1 - p2
    return [round(d - _m.sqrt((p1 - l1) ** 2 + (u2 - p2) ** 2), 4), round(d + _m.sqrt((u1 - p1) ** 2 + (p2 - l2) ** 2), 4)]


def rate(rows: list[dict], k_of, n_of) -> dict:
    """``{point, interval, units}``: the weighted ratio Σ w k / Σ w n and its Korn–Graubard 95 % interval. The
    variance is the design-based one of :func:`ratio` (linearised within the design cells, ``n/(n − 1)``, no
    finite-population correction: the rate is the error process's, the one a regression inherits); the
    design effect of the weights is taken over the units of the rate's own denominator (Kish, unit-weighted:
    Σn · Σ n w² / (Σ n w)²)."""
    import math as _m
    units_rows = [(r, n_of(r)) for r in rows if n_of(r) > 0]
    if not units_rows:
        return {"point": None, "interval": [None, None], "units": 0}
    point, se = ratio([(r["w"], k_of(r), n, r["cell"], 0.0) for r, n in units_rows])
    variance = 0.0 if se is None or _m.isnan(se) else se * se
    n_total = sum(n for _r, n in units_rows)
    deff = n_total * sum(n * r["w"] ** 2 for r, n in units_rows) / sum(n * r["w"] for r, n in units_rows) ** 2
    return {"point": round(point, 4), "interval": korn_graubard(point, variance, n_total, deff), "units": n_total}


RATES = {   # rate → (k, n) per entry; F1 = TP / (TP + (FP + FN) / 2)
    "exact": (lambda r: r["exact"], lambda r: 1.0),
    "precision": (lambda r: r["tp"], lambda r: r["held"]),
    "recall": (lambda r: r["tp"], lambda r: r["marked"]),
    "f1": (lambda r: r["tp"], lambda r: (r["held"] + r["marked"]) / 2),
}


def intervals(key: list[dict], answers: dict) -> dict:
    """Per volume and domain (all, stratum, language, status): each rate's point estimate and Korn–Graubard
    interval (:func:`rate`); and the comparisons D-226 promised — language against language and town against
    other, inside each volume — as the difference with Newcombe's interval (the domains are unions of disjoint
    design cells, so they are independent)."""
    rows = _entry_rows(key, answers)
    out = {}
    for ed in EDITIONS:
        mine = [r for r in rows if r["edition"] == ed]
        if not mine:
            continue
        block = {}
        domains = [("all", mine)] + [(f"{f}={v}", [r for r in mine if r[f] == v])
                                    for f in ("stratum", "language", "status") for v in sorted({r[f] for r in mine})]
        for name, sub in domains:
            block[name] = {k: rate(sub, k_of, n_of) for k, (k_of, n_of) in RATES.items()}
        comparisons = {}
        for field in ("language", "status"):
            values = sorted({r[field] for r in mine} - {"unknown"})
            for i, a in enumerate(values):
                for b in values[i + 1:]:
                    for k in RATES:
                        ra, rb = block[f"{field}={a}"][k], block[f"{field}={b}"][k]
                        if ra["point"] is None or rb["point"] is None:
                            continue
                        lo, hi = newcombe((ra["point"], ra["interval"]), (rb["point"], rb["interval"]))
                        comparisons[f"{field}: {a} - {b}: {k}"] = {
                            "difference": round(ra["point"] - rb["point"], 4), "interval": [lo, hi],
                            "excludes_zero": bool(lo > 0 or hi < 0)}
        block["comparisons"] = comparisons
        out[ed] = block
    return out


# The covariates an analysis leans on (the route's sealed-page table, LEXIKON_1910_ROUTE.md) and the two failures
# the sample found; the label is the plate's English name.
TABLE_SYMBOLS = (("[school]", "School"), ("[fire-brigade]", "Fire brigade"), ("[cooperative]", "Registered cooperative"),
                 ("[midwife]", "Midwife"), ("[rc-parish]", "Roman Catholic parish"), ("[post]", "Post office"),
                 ("[castle]", "Castle or chateau"), ("[poorhouse]", "Welfare institution"),
                 ("[savings-bank]", "Savings bank"), ("[tourist-hut]", "Tourist hut"))
GROUPS = (("cz", "Czech volume"), ("de", r"M\"ahren and Schlesien"))


def covariates(key: list[dict], answers: dict, fixed: dict | None = None, symbols=TABLE_SYMBOLS) -> dict:
    """Per covariate and volume group (Czech; Mähren and Schlesien together), at the entry level the analysis
    uses: weighted sensitivity (printed → held), specificity (not printed → not held) and precision (held →
    printed) on ``answers``, each with its Korn–Graubard interval (:func:`rate`), and the raw counts behind
    them; with ``fixed`` (the adjudicated rulings) also the false and missed counts the second look leaves."""
    rows = _entry_rows(key, answers)
    adjudicated = _entry_rows(key, fixed) if fixed is not None else None
    out = {}
    for group, _name in GROUPS:
        mine = [r for r in rows if r["group"] == group]
        for token, _label in symbols:
            h = lambda r, t=token: t in r["held_set"]          # noqa: E731
            p = lambda r, t=token: t in r["marked_set"]        # noqa: E731
            parts = {"sensitivity": (lambda r: float(h(r) and p(r)), lambda r: float(p(r))),
                     "specificity": (lambda r: float(not h(r) and not p(r)), lambda r: float(not p(r))),
                     "precision": (lambda r: float(h(r) and p(r)), lambda r: float(h(r)))}
            cell = {"entries": len(mine), "printed": sum(p(r) for r in mine), "held": sum(h(r) for r in mine),
                    "found": sum(h(r) and p(r) for r in mine), "false": sum(h(r) and not p(r) for r in mine)}
            for name, (k_of, n_of) in parts.items():
                cell[name] = rate(mine, k_of, n_of)
            if adjudicated is not None:               # what the second look leaves of the disagreements, unweighted
                adj = [r for r in adjudicated if r["group"] == group]
                cell["adjudicated_false"] = sum(h(r) and not p(r) for r in adj)
                cell["adjudicated_missed"] = sum(p(r) and not h(r) for r in adj)
            out[f"{group}:{token}"] = cell
    return out


def frame_coverage(recheck: Path = RECHECK, register: Path = REGISTER) -> dict:
    """Per volume, the share of the anchored municipalities (``settlement_id``) the frame holds, by quintile of
    1910 population (smallest first): what the rates cover. The frame leaves out the pages the author ruled or
    saw and the sealed, reserve, calibration and gold pages, and these weigh on the larger places."""
    m = pd.read_parquet(Path(register) / "register_municipalities.parquet")
    out = {}
    for ed in EDITIONS:
        f = frame(ed, recheck, register)
        keys = set(zip(f["court_key"].astype(str), f["muni_seq"].astype(str)))
        a = m[(m["edition"] == ed) & m["settlement_id"].notna()].copy()
        a["in_frame"] = [(str(c), str(s)) in keys for c, s in zip(a["court_id"], a["muni_seq"])]
        a["q"] = pd.qcut(a["pop_total"].rank(method="first"), 5, labels=False)
        out[ed] = {"frame": int(a["in_frame"].sum()), "anchored": len(a),
                   "share_by_population_quintile": [round(float(v), 2) for v in a.groupby("q")["in_frame"].mean()]}
    return out

def _cell(r: dict, digits: int = 1) -> str:
    """``98.6 [97.4, 99.3]``: the weighted point estimate and its Korn–Graubard 95 % interval in per cent; empty
    when there is nothing to estimate."""
    if r["point"] is None:
        return ""
    lo, hi = r["interval"]
    return f"{100 * r['point']:.{digits}f} [{100 * lo:.{digits}f}, {100 * hi:.{digits}f}]"


def performance_frame(result: dict) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """One row per volume for the blind ruling (primary) and one for the adjudicated ruling (a sensitivity
    analysis): entries ruled; entries exactly right, precision, recall and F1, weighted, with 95 % intervals;
    and the §6d count with its verdict."""
    rows = []

            i = band[ed]["all"]

                         "Recall": _cell(i["recall"], 2), "F1": _cell(i["f1"], 2),
